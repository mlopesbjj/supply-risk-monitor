The Problem

In most companies, supplier delays are discovered too late — when the customer is already calling or the delivery is already late.

  • Late deliveries
  • Stressed sales teams
  • Unhappy customers
  • Loss of trust

The Solution

Supply Risk Monitor automatically highlights sales orders that are at risk due to supplier delays.

  • Automatically detects supply risks
  • Highlights risky orders visually
  • Focused view for immediate action

How It Works

  1. Odoo already knows your sales and purchase dates
  2. If supplier delivery is after the promised sales date
  3. The sales order is marked as at risk
